SECTION 3: PLAN CATEGORIES OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Day1 Health offers 4 main plan categories:

1. DAY-TO-DAY PLAN — Covers everyday medical needs (doctor visits, medication,
   dentistry, optometry). One tier only.

2. HOSPITAL PLANS — Covers private hospital stays, accidents, and emergencies.
   Three tiers: Value Plus, Platinum, Executive.

3. COMPREHENSIVE PLANS — Combines Day-to-Day AND Hospital benefits in one plan.
   Three tiers: Value Plus, Platinum, Executive.

4. SENIOR PLANS — Designed specifically for senior members.
   Three sub-types: Senior Day-to-Day, Senior Hospital, Senior Comprehensive.
   Senior plans do NOT offer child cover.

Subcategory Options (for non-senior plans):
- Single (1 adult)
- Couple (2 adults)
- Family (1 or 2 adults + up to 4 children aged 2–11)

Subcategory Options (for senior plans):
- Single (1 adult)
- Couple (2 adults)
- No family/child options available on Senior plans.

Hospital and Comprehensive plan tiers (from lowest to highest):
- Value Plus: Entry-level hospital/comprehensive cover.
- Platinum: Adds Critical Illness and Accidental Permanent Disability.
- Executive: Highest tier — adds Illness Top-Up, enhanced daily benefits, and
  higher accident/trauma limits.

SECTION 11: PROCEDURES & HOW-TO GUIDES
================================================================================

--- HOW TO JOIN ---
1. View plans and pricing at https://day1health.co.za (or call 0876 100 600).
2. Choose a plan (Day-to-Day, Hospital, Comprehensive, or Senior).
3. Apply online or by phone (quick 5-minute application).
4. Cover starts immediately for accidents and emergency ambulance.
5. Welcome Pack sent after first successful debit order.

--- WELCOME PACK ---
After your first successful debit order (including once-off activation fee),
you will receive:
- Day1 Health membership card
- Welcome letter
- Africa Assist (A-A) decal (car) sticker
- Policy schedule and endorsements (if applicable)
- Product guide
- Policy wording (for hospital policies only)

Activation: Membership activated within 24 hours. Confirmation SMS sent with
membership number and contact info.

--- CHRONIC MEDICATION PROCEDURE ---
1. Get diagnosed by your Day1 Health Network Partner.
2. Register on the Day1 Health Chronic Disease Management Programme.
3. Collect chronic medication from Clicks, Dischem, or Medirite nationwide.
4. Subject to pre-authorisation.
5. An additional administration fee may apply.
6. Download the Chronic Application Form from the website or request via email/phone.

--- CLAIMS PROCEDURE ---
GP visits and prescribed medication within the network are covered directly.

For out-of-area GP consultations, minor procedures, or medication reimbursement:
1. Download the Day1 Health Reimbursement Form from the website.
2. Attach: Copy of detailed account, proof of payment, proof of bank details.
3. Submit within 120 days (4 months) from date of service.
4. Payments are made within 30 days via EFT only.
5. Claims older than 4 months will be rejected as stale.

--- HOSPITAL EMERGENCY PROCEDURE ---
1. ALWAYS call Africa-Assist first: 0861 144 144.
2. Tell the operator you are a Day1 Health member.
3. Have your membership number or ID number ready.
4. All admissions (accident AND illness) MUST be authorised.

Tips:
- Teach family members to call 0861 144 144 in emergencies.
- In accidents, note road names/numbers to speed up response.
- Save 0861 144 144 under "Medical Emergency" on your phone.

--- RESERVATION OF RIGHTS ---
Day1 Health reserves the right to cancel membership if a member:
- Provides false information or fails to disclose medical info.
- Submits fraudulent claims.
- Allows unauthorised persons to use their membership card.
- Fails to report services not delivered but paid for.
- Commits any other fraudulent act.
- Fails to pay monthly contributions/premiums.

SECTION 12: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
================================================================================

Q: Can I go to any private hospital?
A: Day1 Health has formal contracts with Life Healthcare, Mediclinic, Africa Health
Care and Clinix hospitals. Admission into Netcare is at their discretion nationwide.
All hospital admissions must be pre-authorised via Africa Assist (0861 144 144).

Q: What is the procedure if I'm diagnosed with a chronic condition?
A: Register on the Day1 Health Chronic Disease Management Programme via your Day1
Health Network Partner to receive chronic medication. Collect at Clicks, Dischem or
Medirite nationwide. Subject to pre-authorisation.

Q: Can I buy my medication over the counter?
A: No. Your Day1 Network Partner must prescribe in line with the formulary. Both acute
and chronic medication are covered according to the formulary. If your GP scripts
(does not dispense), collect at Clicks, Dischem or Medirite.

Q: When I see a GP/Dentist, do I have to pay cash?
A: No. By paying your monthly contribution in advance, your consultation is covered.
Accredited partners claim directly from Day1 Health.

Q: What happens if there isn't a Network doctor in my area?
A: Contact us on 0876 100 600 and we will endeavour to contract directly with your
preferred GP.

Q: What happens if I am ill and out of town?
A: Your Day-to-Day benefits allow 3 out-of-area visits per policy per year to an
alternative Network Partner or GP of your choice. For non-Network Partner visits,
submit a Reimbursement Form and receipt to be reimbursed the agreed tariff (e.g. R340).

Q: Are pre-existing conditions covered?
A: Yes, after a 12-month waiting period.

Q: What are the waiting periods for day-to-day benefits?
A: GP/Acute/Pathology/Radiology/Out-of-Area: 1 month.
   Specialists/Dentistry: 3 months.
   Chronic (new conditions): 3 months.
   Chronic (pre-existing): 12 months.
   Optometry: 12 months.
